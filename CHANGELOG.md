# Changelog

All notable public product changes are documented here. Crystal Flip uses [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/) for release references where applicable.

## [3.0.1] - Service Update

### Improved
- Faster scanning with reduced detection latency across supported platforms.
- Faster developer-history loading and more accurate historical MCAP calculations.
- Refined detection filters to reduce noise and emphasize higher-quality candidates.
- General application performance and stability.

### Added
- Detection history for reviewing recent candidates and comparing opportunities.

### Fixed
- Application issues reported during the maintenance window.
- A purchase-time configuration issue; affected users were instructed to reinstall through the official bot.

## [3.0.0] - 2026-03-03

### Added
- Crystal AI Score with a dynamic 0–100 rating and factor breakdown.
- Developer Behavioral Analytics 2.0 and recurring behavior profiles.
- Liquidity Intelligence with risk and artificial-volume signals.
- Twitter Deep Intelligence 3.0, engagement analysis, and Narrative Heatmap.
- Community Power Index and Admin Influence Score.
- Smart Auto-Mode with configurable entry filters.
- Portfolio and filter-performance analytics.
- Anti-manipulation indicators and recurring scam-network detection.
- Distributed scanning and smarter recurring-entity caching.
