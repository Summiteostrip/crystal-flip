# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is Crystal Flip?

Crystal Flip is a proprietary real-time research platform for newly launched tokens. It combines market scanning, developer history, Community analytics, Twitter intelligence, risk signals, and configurable automatic opening.

## Is Crystal Flip open source?

No. The software, scoring implementation, server code, and internal algorithms are proprietary. This private repository contains product documentation and official visual materials.

## Which networks are supported?

Published product materials describe support for Solana and BNB Chain. Venue and feature coverage may differ between networks and releases.

## Does Crystal Flip trade automatically?

The documented automation opens token candidates based on configurable rules. It should not be confused with a guarantee of execution, profit, or token safety. Review the current product controls before enabling any automation.

## What is Crystal AI Score?

It is a dynamic 0–100 analytical rating derived from supported developer, social, liquidity, behavioral, and growth signals. It is a heuristic and not financial advice or a guarantee of future performance.

## Why can historical data be incomplete?

External APIs may be unavailable or inconsistent, old launches may lack data, and coverage varies by venue. Market-cap values can differ between providers.
