# Product Direction

Crystal Flip is actively developed. This page describes broad product direction, not binding delivery dates.

## Reliability and Coverage

- Reduce detection latency and external-data inconsistencies.
- Improve historical accuracy across supported networks and venues.
- Expand monitoring resilience and recurring-entity caching.

## Decision Quality

- Refine AI Score explainability and configurable factor weighting.
- Improve developer behavior, liquidity, and manipulation signals.
- Reduce duplicate and low-quality detections.

## Research Workflow

- Expand detection review and strategy-performance analysis.
- Improve notes, filters, importable settings, and workspace usability.
- Make complex analytics easier to interpret during fast-moving launches.

## Social Intelligence

- Improve engagement-quality and historian-trust analysis.
- Develop narrative monitoring and contextual Twitter analysis.
- Explore automatic translation for monitored tweets.

## Community Feedback

Feature suggestions and configuration feedback can be submitted through [@CrystalFlip_bot](https://t.me/CrystalFlip_bot). Priorities may change based on data availability, security, reliability, and customer needs.
