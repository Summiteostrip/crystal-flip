# Risk and Data Limitations

## Market Risk

Newly launched tokens are highly speculative. Prices and liquidity can change rapidly, and a complete loss is possible. Historical developer, Community, or social-account performance does not guarantee a similar future result.

## Analytics Are Not Certainties

AI Scores, trust indices, rug warnings, fake-volume indicators, and behavioral classifications are analytical heuristics. They can be incomplete or wrong and should never be the sole basis for a decision.

A high score does not establish that a token is safe. A low score does not prove malicious intent.

## External Data Limitations

Crystal Flip depends on external platforms and public data sources. Consequently:

- APIs may be delayed, unavailable, or inconsistent.
- Market-cap calculations can differ between providers.
- Historical coverage can vary by network and launch venue.
- Old launches may have incomplete information.
- Social metrics may not accurately represent genuine engagement.
- A participant can change wallets or accounts to avoid historical profiling.

Official materials specifically acknowledge possible Pump.fun-related inaccuracies and incomplete previous-token data in some cases.

## Automation Risk

Automatic opening reduces reaction time but does not validate a token. Misconfigured filters, stale data, source errors, or unexpected market conditions can produce unwanted results.

Test settings with manual review before enabling automation. Use appropriate limits and independent confirmation.

## Security Practices

- Use only the official Telegram channel and bot listed in this repository.
- Do not share passwords, seed phrases, private keys, or payment credentials with support personnel.
- Verify downloaded files and instructions through the official bot.
- Keep API credentials and private configuration out of documentation and screenshots.
- Treat unsolicited direct messages and lookalike accounts as potential impersonation.
